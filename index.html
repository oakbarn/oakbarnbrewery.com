Skynet Brewer

Skynet Brewer runs your home brewery from any web browser: Windows, Mac, Linux, Android or iPhone. A Raspberry Pi or small PC is the brain, and it talks to the boards (Arduino, ESP32, Raspberry Pi) that switch your pumps, valves and heaters.

It is free.
